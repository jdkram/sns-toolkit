Volunteer statuses

Every volunteer has one status. Status controls login access, rota visibility, and data-retention eligibility.

StatusCountLoginHow they get hereHow they leave

Auto-dormancy (no login for {{ dormancy_days|days_to_human|default:"N/A" }}{% if never_logged_in_grace_days %}, or {{ never_logged_in_grace_days|days_to_human }} if never logged in{% endif %}); or panopticon sets Dormant / Retired / Suspended.

Retired {{ status_counts.retired|default:"0" }} Login still works Panopticon only. Panopticon only (→ Active / Dormant). Eligible for anonymisation once past retention window. No self-service — retirement is a deliberate admin decision.
Suspended {{ status_counts.suspended|default:"0" }} Login disabled. Future rota slots released. Panopticon only (reason required). Used for access-control or welfare situations requiring immediate login removal. Panopticon only (→ Active / Dormant / Retired). Note: cleared rota slots are not restored. No self-service.
Anonymised {{ status_counts.anonymised|default:"0" }} Login disabled. PII erased. Panopticon action (individual or bulk) once past the {{ purge_days|days_to_human }} retention window. Irreversible. This is effectively our “delete” — it wipes all personal data while keeping rota history under a placeholder name, for accountability.

Self-service reactivation applies to Dormant only. Retired and Suspended require a panopticon to reverse.

Before you copy these addresses

Always use BCC, not To or CC.

These are personal email addresses. Sending to multiple people in the To or CC field reveals everyone's address to everyone else, which is a privacy breach. Put the addresses in BCC, and use a placeholder in the To field (such as your own address).

If you are not sure how to use BCC in your email client, please check before sending.
