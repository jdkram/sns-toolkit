How film readiness works
  • Ready once any one copy has been test-played successfully, so a working backup covers a failed main copy.
  • Copies are a main copy and any backups. One film can play at several showings; a double bill is two films on one showing.
  • Steps depend on the copy's format (below). Anyone who programmes or projects can claim one, and each records who did it and when.
  • Hired hard drives have two extra steps after the last showing (sent back, confirmed). They don't affect readiness, but stay under "Hard drives to send back" until confirmed, and for {{ return_keep_days }} days after.
  • Nothing is one-way. A claim can be handed back and a finished step marked not done, from the film's Event Hub panel. Both are logged against the event with who did it.
  • Flags: a film not yet ready is marked "soon" within {{ lookahead_days }} days and "urgent" within {{ urgent_days }}.
