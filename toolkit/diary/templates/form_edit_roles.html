  • Archiving a role hides it from rota dropdowns and volunteer profiles. Past rota entries are preserved. Roles used in past rota entries are automatically archived rather than deleted.
  • Roles marked read-only are protected and cannot be archived or deleted here.
  • Renaming a role changes its name retroactively everywhere: all past rota entries, templates, and qualification gates will display the new name immediately. This is usually what you want when correcting a typo or tidying terminology. If you want a genuinely new role with its own history, create a new one instead.
