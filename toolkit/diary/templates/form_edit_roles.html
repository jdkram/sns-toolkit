Roles

Panopticon-only page.
  • Deleting a role is permanent and cascades silently. It will immediately and irreversibly remove that role from every rota assignment across all past and future showings, and from every event template. There is no confirmation step and no undo.
  • Roles marked read-only (e.g. Projectionist) are protected and cannot be deleted here.
  • Renaming a role is safe. The name is stored once and referenced everywhere relationally, so a rename takes effect immediately on all showings, templates, and volunteer profiles — no manual updates needed.

Events marked as "Standard Event Roles" are shown at the top of the role selection list.

Add new roles at the end of the list.
