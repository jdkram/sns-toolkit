Title: **Venue programming form - volunteers only**

## Preamble

This form is for all volunteers wishing to put on an event within the venue as a whole - including cinema use outside of our regular programming.
- If you want to propose a film in our regular (Thursday/Friday/Sunday at 7.30pm) cinema programme, please join the Film Programming Collective!
- Private hires should go through our 'hire and enquire' page on the website - https://www.starandshadow.org.uk/hire-and-enquire/enquire/

Before bringing any venue programming item to a Monday meeting for approval, please familiarise yourself with the programming etiquette guide (linked, internal Nextcloud file) and ensure this form is completed in full.

The information provided here populates a spreadsheet, which is used to track events and will be reviewed at relevant Monday meetings.

You can come to a Monday meeting and observe or ask a question before completing this form, and we encourage this!

We appreciate that this form asks for a lot of information. This is realistically what we need to approve an event; to ensure that we can fully accommodate and that we can all engage cooperatively.

If you are unable to attend the meeting please complete this form in full and arrange a peer to bring it on your behalf.

**Accessibility:** If you have any access requirements that impact your capacity to use this form, please ask a peer or Star and Shadow volunteer to help you. Paper copies can also be located [location TBD].

**Notes:**
- If all requested information has not been provided, your event cannot be approved.
- Monday meetings can only approve events up to £500, anything over must follow the £500+ process.
- IMPORTANT: "profit" = ticket sales only, never bar takings (bar overheads are separate: taxis for vols, stock, gas, electricity, food for vols, cleaning products etc).

Responses are connected to the submitter's Nextcloud account. Asterisk = mandatory.
